Dealer floor plan finance

Stock finance for independent dealers that large lenders overlook.

Luvium Capital finances vehicle stock for independent dealerships in South Africa, including rural and underserved regions. Each advance is secured against a specific vehicle and monitored until it is sold.

Solution

How floor plan finance works with Luvium

The dealer buys stock with Luvium finance, sells the vehicle, and repays the advance with interest. Until then, the vehicle is the security.

  1. Fund. Luvium finances the purchase of specific vehicles for a vetted dealer, up to an approved facility limit. Facility size to confirm
  2. Sell. The dealer sells the vehicle to a retail customer and the sale is recorded against the advance.
  3. Repay. The advance and interest are repaid on sale. Ageing stock is flagged early and audited.

Dealer criteria (indicative)

  • At least two years of trading history
  • Located in rural or underserved regions, or poorly served by large lenders
  • Verifiable monthly sales volume minimum to confirm
  • Sound financial records and a record of compliance

Risk and control

Security is attached to each vehicle.

Funders ask about controls first, so this is where we start. The points below describe how the product is designed to work.

Per-vehicle security

Every advance is tied to an identified vehicle, so the exposure behind each rand is known.

Active monitoring

Stock audits and ageing alerts run through our platform to catch problems early.

Dealer vetting

Dealers are assessed on trading history, stock turnover and location before a line is opened.

Clear reporting

Funding partners receive regular statements and portfolio reporting.
