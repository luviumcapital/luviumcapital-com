Funding partners

Deal flow, collateral control and servicing for funding partners.

Luvium Capital originates and manages floor plan finance for independent vehicle dealers across South Africa. We bring vetted dealers, vehicle-level security and day-to-day servicing, so a partner can deploy capital against vehicles without building the operation.

Start a partner conversation

Who we work with

Partners who want exposure to a defined, asset-backed niche.

Large lenders serve this segment poorly. We are looking for partners across four groups.

Funders and co-lenders

Wholesale funds, specialist lenders and family offices seeking secured, short-tenor, vehicle-backed exposure.

Insurers and guarantors

Cover for stock, credit and settlement risk across a dealer book.

Data and verification providers

Vehicle history, valuation, tracking and dealer vetting that feed our approval and monitoring.

Dealer networks and OEM channels

Groups and associations that want finance available to their independent dealers.

What a partner gets

Capabilities a partner can verify in due diligence.

Confirm each point against the live process before publishing.

Vetted origination

Dealers assessed on sales history, stock turnover and location before any line is opened.

Per-vehicle security

Each drawdown is tied to a specific vehicle, so the partner knows what backs the exposure.

Active monitoring

Stock audits, ageing alerts and repayment on sale, run on our platform.

Full servicing

Statements, collections and reporting handled by Luvium, with clear regular reporting to the partner.

Pilot path

How a partnership works.

  1. Introduction. We share dealer criteria, product terms and our risk framework under NDA.
  2. Pilot facility. A small, capped pool with a handful of dealers, to test the process end to end.
  3. Review. Joint review of performance, losses and operations against agreed measures.
  4. Scale. Facility size and dealer count grow as the pilot proves out.
