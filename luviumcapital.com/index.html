Luvium Capital was founded on the principle that every viable business deserves access to growth capital. We recognized a critical gap in the South African market: thousands of vehicle dealerships in rural and underserved areas unable to access the floor plan financing they need to thrive.

Our Solution

A comprehensive floor plan financing solution tailored for underserved vehicle dealerships

The Challenge

South Africa's used vehicle market is valued at over R20 billion annually, yet thousands of legitimate dealerships in rural and underserved areas lack access to floor plan financing. Traditional lenders focus on established urban dealers, leaving a significant market segment without the capital needed to stock inventory and grow their businesses.

This creates a lose-lose situation: dealerships cannot reach their potential, communities lack access to quality vehicles, and investors miss out on attractive risk-adjusted returns in an underserved market.

How It Works

  1. Fund

    Luvium provides R3.5M in floor plan financing to qualified dealerships, enabling them to purchase and stock quality used vehicles.

  2. Grow

    Dealerships use the capital to expand their inventory, increase sales, and grow their businesses while serving their communities.

  3. Repay

    As vehicles are sold, dealerships repay the financing with interest, generating consistent returns for investors.

Target Dealership Characteristics

  • Established track record (2+ years operating)
  • Located in underserved or rural regions
  • Minimum monthly sales of R500,000
  • Strong community reputation and relationships
  • Demonstrated financial management capabilities
  • Commitment to compliance and transparency

Risk Management

Protecting investor capital through disciplined origination, control of collateral and ongoing monitoring.

  • Asset control

    Every advance is tied to a specific vehicle, so the security behind each rand is identified and controlled.

  • Dealer vetting

    Dealers are assessed on sales history, inventory turnover and market location before a line is opened.

  • Stock monitoring

    Stock audits and ageing alerts help us spot slow-moving inventory early.

  • Insurance coverage

    Insurance is part of the risk framework, protecting the financed stock.

  • Repayment on sale

    As each vehicle is sold, the financing is repaid with interest, keeping exposure tied to stock that is actually on the floor.

  • Clear reporting

    Regular statements and portfolio reporting give investors a clear view of exposure and performance.

Common questions

What secures each advance?

Each advance is secured against the specific vehicle it finances. The dealer's inventory forms the security for the credit line.

How do dealers qualify?

Target dealerships have an established track record (2+ years operating), are located in underserved or rural regions, have minimum monthly sales of R500,000, a strong community reputation, demonstrated financial management capabilities and a commitment to compliance and transparency.

How large is a facility?

Luvium provides R3.5M in floor plan financing to qualified dealerships.
