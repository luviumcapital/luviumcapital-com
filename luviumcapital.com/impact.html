Our ESG Framework

Environmental, Social, and Governance principles are embedded in every aspect of our business model and investment approach.

  • Social Impact

    Creating meaningful employment and supporting local communities: job creation in underserved areas; SME formalization and growth; skills development and training; community economic empowerment.

  • Economic Development

    Driving inclusive economic growth and transformation: local spend stimulation; financial inclusion advancement; market access improvement; value chain strengthening.

  • Governance Excellence

    Maintaining highest standards of corporate governance: transparent reporting practices; robust risk management; regulatory compliance; stakeholder engagement.

Our Development Objectives

  • Strengthening Local Economies

    By providing working capital to underserved dealerships, we enable them to increase inventory, expand operations, and contribute more significantly to their local economies. Increased local procurement and spending; enhanced tax base for municipalities; improved economic multiplier effects.

  • Stimulating Employment

    Our financing enables dealerships to expand their operations, creating direct employment opportunities and supporting indirect job creation throughout the automotive value chain. Direct employment at dealership level; support services and suppliers; skills development and training opportunities.
