Who we work with

We are looking for partners who want exposure to a defined, asset-backed niche that large lenders serve poorly.

Funders and co-lenders

Wholesale funds, specialist lenders and family offices that want secured, short-tenor, vehicle-backed exposure.

Insurers and guarantors

Cover for stock, credit and settlement risk across a dealer book.

Data and verification providers

Vehicle history, valuation, tracking and dealer vetting that feed our approval and monitoring.

Dealer networks and OEM channels

Groups and associations that want finance available to their independent dealers.

What a partner gets

Each point below is a capability the partner can verify in due diligence.

Confirm each against the live process before publishing.

Vetted origination

Dealers assessed on sales history, stock turnover and location before any line is opened.

Per-vehicle security

Each drawdown is tied to a specific vehicle, so the partner always knows what backs the exposure.

Active monitoring

Stock audits, ageing alerts and repayment on sale, run on our platform.

Full servicing

Statements, collections and reporting handled by Luvium. The partner receives clear, regular reporting.

How a partnership works

  1. Introduction.

    We share our dealer criteria, product terms and risk framework under NDA.

  2. Pilot facility.

    A small, capped pool with a handful of dealers, to test the process end to end.

  3. Review.

    Joint review of performance, losses and operations against agreed measures.

  4. Scale.

    Facility size and dealer count grow as the pilot proves out.
