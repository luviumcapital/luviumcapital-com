Privacy notice

Draft for review by counsel before publication.

Luvium Holdings (Pty) Ltd ("Luvium", "we") operates luviumcapital.com. This notice explains what personal information we collect through the site and how we use it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact or partner enquiry forms: your name, email address, organisation, the kind of enquiry and your message. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Your colour mode choice is stored in your browser so the site remembers it.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to start a conversation about investment documents or a funding partnership. We do not sell your information.

Who sees it

Luvium staff and the email and hosting providers that carry your message to us. We share it with others only where the law requires.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting relationship, and then for the period the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may object to its use. Email admin@luviumcapital.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
